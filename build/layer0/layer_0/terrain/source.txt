Terrain Data Source:

Primary elevation data source:
USGS SRTM 1 Arc-Second Global

Source:
USGS Earth Resources Observation and Science (EROS) Center
https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/usgs-eros-archive-digital-elevation-shuttle-radar-topography-mission-srtm-1

DEM file:
N34_E077_1arc_v3.tif

Location:
Leh, Ladakh
Latitude: 34.16
Longitude: 77.58

Spatial resolution:
1 arc-second (approximately 30 m)

Coordinate reference:
WGS 84

Elevation units:
metres

Terrain processing:
A 36-direction horizon profile was generated from the DEM at 10-degree azimuth intervals, covering 0° to 350°.

Output:
horizon_36dir.csv

A coarse terrain mesh was generated from the same DEM.

Output:
terrain_mesh.obj

Note:
The same SRTM elevation dataset is used for both the horizon profile and the coarse terrain mesh to maintain consistency between terrain calculations.
