Primary source:
ASHRAE Handbook — Fundamentals, Chapter 26, Heat, Air, and Moisture Control in Building Assemblies: Material Properties.

The density, thermal conductivity (k), and specific heat (Cp) values in materials.csv are taken from or based on the corresponding material entries in the ASHRAE material-property tables.

These are generic material properties. Actual properties may vary with composition, density, moisture content, and temperature.

Cost, local availability, transport penalty, and mason skill are not treated as measured material properties and should be replaced with locally sourced values before being used in final simulations.
